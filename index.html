盤點Stocktake

    
  

  
企喺雪櫃前面，見到樣嘢仲喺度就撳一下（變綠 ✓）；搵唔到就撳兩下（變紅 ✗）。唔記得就唔好撳，留空最安全。撳格仔下面嗰個日期，就可以核對返實物上面嘅日期；如果係新買嘅同款嘢，即場換新一件。做到一半可以閂咗佢，下次開返會問你要唔要繼續。Stand at the fridge and go through the pictures. Tap a picture once if that food is still there — it turns green with a ✓. Tap it twice if you cannot find it — it turns red with a ✗. If you are not sure, leave it alone: anything untouched is kept exactly as it is. Tap the date underneath a picture to check it against the date printed on the real package. If it is a new pack of the same food, that button replaces it with a new one for you. You can close this halfway — next time it will offer to carry on where you stopped.

  

  





  
  








  

  

    
可能重複咗Possible duplicates

    
  

  
撳張相可以全屏放大，睇清楚價錢／重量貼紙上面嘅數字 —— 兩底貨嘅貼紙一定唔同，呢個先至分得開。名一樣、到期日差三日之內就會攞出嚟問你，睇埋邊個影、幾時影，通常一眼就知。Tap a photo to see it full screen and read the numbers on the price or weight sticker — two separate packs always carry different stickers, and that is what really tells them apart. Items are listed here when they share a name and their expiry dates are within three days of each other. Also look at who logged each one and when; that usually settles it at a glance.

Whoever taps Thrown out is doing a chore, not doing something wrong: if in doubt, throw it out.
